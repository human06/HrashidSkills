Three groups

Every enterprise build answers to three groups.

Each is accountable for something different — and nothing reaches production unless all three are satisfied.

Businessoutcome pressure

Did this move the number — and when?

  • Time to market
  • Return on investment
  • Predictable cost
  • Regulatory compliance
Architectsintegrity pressure

Will this still stand in five years?

  • Security & data integrity
  • Scale and performance
  • Fits the existing estate
  • Standards, not one-offs
Engineersdelivery pressure

Can I actually build and run this?

  • Clear, stable requirements
  • Tooling and environments
  • Testable and debuggable
  • Realistic scope and time

Satisfy only two of the three and a project doesn’t half-succeed. It stops.
