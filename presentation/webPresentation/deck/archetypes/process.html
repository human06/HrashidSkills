Rollout · illustrative workflow

Four stages take an idea to production.

Each stage has a clear outcome and an owner.

01

Define the use case

The business owner sets the goal and the acceptance criteria.

02

Build a pilot

The delivery team implements a small end-to-end workflow.

03

Verify the controls

Reviewers check evidence for access, policy and reliability.

04

Release and monitor

The service owner follows outcomes and handles exceptions.

The handoffs are explicit at every stage.
