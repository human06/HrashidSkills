# Cinematic Decks

Story-driven HTML presentations: one world, one camera, told in acts. This is the language shared by the `deck` skill, its reference files, and the conversations that shape a deck.

## The story

**Brief**:
What a new deck starts from: a topic, audience, goal and length, given in conversation or as a free-form markdown file.
_Avoid_: Prompt, spec, requirements

**Storyboard**:
The deck on paper (acts, frames, headlines, steps and takeaways), approved before any HTML exists.
_Avoid_: Outline, plan, script

**Act**:
A named chapter of the story: a run of consecutive frames along the world's path, announced by an act card. Every deck runs Prologue → Acts → Epilogue.
_Avoid_: Section, chapter, part

**Lit phrase**:
The one highlighted phrase in a frame's headline that carries its idea.
_Avoid_: Highlight, accent words

**Takeaway**:
The closing line a frame lands on after its last step.
_Avoid_: Summary, key message, conclusion

**Finale**:
The closing shot where the camera pulls back over the whole world and the deck's last line appears.
_Avoid_: Outro, ending, closing slide

## The deck

**Deck**:
The single self-contained HTML file that is presented.
_Avoid_: Presentation file, slideshow

**World**:
The one large canvas every frame sits on, which the camera travels across.
_Avoid_: Canvas, board, map

**Frame**:
One slide placed in the world. "Slide" is fine in conversation.
_Avoid_: Page, screen

**Step**:
One click inside a frame that reveals the next part of its build-up.
_Avoid_: Build, animation step, fragment

**Shot**:
Where the camera sits for a given step: framing the whole frame, or pushed in on a detail.
_Avoid_: View, zoom level, camera state

**Archetype**:
A proven, reusable frame design (such as the gate with a passing and a blocked run) that a frame can be built from.
_Avoid_: Template, layout, slide type
